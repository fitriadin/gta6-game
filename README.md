## Features

- **Open city** — Solmar Bay: downtown towers, the Marina, the Beachfront, Palm Heights and Old Town, with traffic, pedestrians, parks and palms.
- **Drive & steal** — take any parked or occupied car, from sedans and taxis to vans and trucks; arcade handling with drifts and skid marks.
- **Police & wanted** — a 1–5 star wanted level; pursuit cars that chase and ram, foot officers who can bust you, and roadblocks at 3+ stars. Lose the heat by staying out of sight.
- **Combat** — mouse-aim pistol (including drive-bys from the car window), F to punch up close, car explosions and a health system with busted/wasted respawns.
- **Story missions** — 6 jobs with briefings, markers on the minimap and in the world, timed deliveries, rival drivers to stop, and a story ending; progress saves between sessions.
- **HUD & save** — health, money, wanted stars, mission objective, speedometer and a live minimap; autosave on pause, continue from the title screen.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts  # then open http://localhost:8080
```

## Controls

WASD/arrows move & drive · E enter/exit/steal car · Mouse aim, click shoot · F punch · Space handbrake/sprint · P pause · M mute
